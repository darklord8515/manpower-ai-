# CONSTRUCTIQ MVP — Plan

## Goal and the demo
The MVP must show HR turning staffing needs into approved, mobilizing workers, and seeing the
problems ahead before they happen: AI recommendations, compliance checks, HR review and approval,
a forward manpower forecast, stateful HR alerts, and automation. Everything else is out of scope.

**HR is the primary user.** HR will test allocation hands-on before the CEO sees it, so every HR
screen must be fully usable (see `docs/design-direction.md` → "HR screens").

### Demo story (HR-led, ~5 minutes)
Seeded state: Thursday 5 Nov 2026 (see "Seed data").
1. **HR opens the HR Console.** The Action Center at the top lists open alerts by severity
   (icon + label + colour), including "RAK-07 starts 12 Feb 2027: 4 masons short" and a visa
   expiring in 7 days for a worker on site.
2. **A Project Manager raises a request** for 8 steel fixers at RAK-03 from Sunday (request form;
   through the Command Centre only if P6 is built).
3. **The Workforce Agent recommends** 8 cleared workers with a reason each, and excludes 2 whose
   visas expire before cover ends.
4. **HR reviews on the Allocation Board,** swaps one worker (validated live) and approves the line.
   Assignments and mobilizations are created in one transaction; department tasks are created; n8n
   sends WhatsApp / email tasks (test number).
5. **HR opens the RAK-07 shortfall alert,** reads the explanation and the facts, and opens the
   **Manpower Forecast:** steel fixers are covered, but "depend on RAK-02 finishing by 5 Feb 2027";
   masons are 4 short. HR presses **Reserve transfers** for the steel fixers and **Raise
   recruitment request** for 4 masons. The detector run triggered by those events auto-resolves the
   shortfall alert.
6. **Operations moves RAK-02's end date** to 20 Feb 2027. A critical "transfer dependency broken"
   alert appears for HR, listing the 8 reserved workers and the new date.
7. **The audit log** shows every step: who did what, which agent recommended what, and which
   detector raised or resolved each alert. (If P5 is built: the CEO overview shows the new counts.)

Screens label what is live, what is demo data, and what is a future integration.

## RBAC
Access = role permissions × data scope. Permissions are `resource:action` strings. Scope is
own / site / project / department / all. Project and site scope come only from
`user_project_access` (one row per project, or per site for supervisors).
All seeded in `db/schema.sql` (roles, permissions, role_permissions).

There is no salary data: the client will not provide it. The sensitive fields are passport and
Emirates ID numbers (the number on the `residency_visa` document, `employee_documents.doc_number`; shown masked, revealed with an eye icon that is audited) and project contract values.

| Role | Employees | Projects | Staffing requests | Mob / demob | Approvals | Forecast / recruitment | Action Center | Passport / EID nos. | Contract value | AI | Agent admin |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Super Admin | R/C all | R/C all | R all | R all | — (Run now automations) | — | — | R | R | Yes | Yes |
| CEO / MD | R all | R all | R/A all | R all | Final tier | R / R | R (counts) | R | R | Yes | Read |
| Operations Manager | R all | R/C all | R/A all | R/C/A all | Staffing, assignments, mobilization | R, reserve / R/C | R, act | — | — | Yes | — |
| HR Manager | R/C all, import | R all | R/A all (Allocation Board) | R/C/A, doc checks | Staffing, assignments, mobilization | R, reserve / R/C | R, act | R | — | Yes | — |
| HR Executive | R/C all | R all | R all (board read-only) | Doc checks | — | R / R | R, act | — | — | HR tools | — |
| Project Manager | R assigned projects + candidate pool | R assigned | R/C assigned | Initiate | — | — | R own projects | — | — | Own projects | — |
| Logistics Coordinator | Narrow projection | R all | — | Beds & transport | — | — | — | — | — | Limited | — |
| Site Supervisor | R own site crew | R own site | R own site | R own site, confirm arrival | — | — | — | — | — | Limited | — |
| Employee | Own | Own assignment | — | Own status | — | — | — | Own | — | — | — |
| n8n service account | — | — | — | — | — | — | Runs detectors | — | — | — | — (`notification:write`, `report:read`, `alert:detect`) |

"Reserve" = Reserve transfers, which needs `assignment:approve`. "Act" = take, assign, snooze,
dismiss (`alert:act`); action buttons inside an alert still need their own permission.

Enforcement:
- FastAPI dependency `require("resource:action")` on every route; frontend hiding is cosmetic only.
- Scope applies to employees, assignments, mobilizations, documents **and alerts**.
- Field masking: `doc_number` unless `employee:read_sensitive` (employees get it with scope `own`,
  so they see their own numbers only); `contract_value_aed` unless `project:read_financial`.
- Logistics Coordinator gets a narrow employee projection: name, employee code, trade, phone,
  camp. Nothing else, whichever endpoint or agent tool returns the employee.
- Candidate pool (approved): anyone with `staffing:create` can search the company-wide available
  pool, seeing only name, employee code, trade, skill level, availability window and a
  `documents_valid` flag. No document numbers, no nationality.
- Approvals: any user with the subject's approve permission and scope can decide a step; one
  person can't decide two steps of the same approval (`docs/rules.md` → Approvals).
- AI agents inherit the calling user's permissions; each tool declares its permission. Agents'
  only write is a draft staffing request; every committed write is a button on a
  permission-checked route.
- Audit every write, approval, alert change and agent tool call (`audit_log`, `agent_tool_calls`
  are append-only).

## Data model
35 tables in `db/schema.sql` (v0.3). Rules enforced by the database:
- No double-booking: exclusion constraint `employee_assignments_no_overlap`. This also protects
  reserved transfers: extending a source assignment into a reservation fails.
- One live alert per condition: unique `dedupe_key` while open / in progress / snoozed.
- Dismissing an alert needs a reason (check); recruitment requests need a reason (check).
- Append-only audit for the app role (default privileges cover tables added by migrations).
- History kept: close records with status / end date, never delete.

## Platform policies
- **Visa / document horizon:** an assignment's documents must stay valid until `needed_until`.
  When `needed_until` is null, check to today + 90 days (`DOC_CHECK_DEFAULT_HORIZON_DAYS=90`).
- **Forecasting:** `TRANSFER_BUFFER_DAYS=7`, `FORECAST_DOC_HORIZON_DAYS=90`,
  `FORECAST_WINDOW_DAYS=120`. All forecast numbers come from the Python forecast service
  (`FORECAST_PYTHON_ONLY`); deployable workers are shared until reserved.
- **Alerts:** `APPROVAL_WAIT_HOURS=24`; escalation steps per severity in `ALERT_ESCALATION`
  (critical 4 h → 8 h, action needed 24 h → 48 h, info never); snooze max 7 days (24 h for
  critical). Detectors run on domain events and at 07:00 and 18:00.
- **Demo reset:** `make reset-demo` drops and recreates the `public` schema, runs the migrations
  (baseline = `db/schema.sql`), then the seed and a detector run. It refuses to run unless
  `APP_ENV` is `local` or `demo`.
- **Files:** behind a `StorageBackend` interface. `local` (filesystem) for development only;
  `s3` (any S3-compatible bucket, R2 in the demo) for every hosted environment. Type and size
  checked on upload; served only through short-lived signed URLs.
- **Shifts:** each site has default shift times; an assignment may override them. The midday-ban
  validator and detector use the effective shift.

## n8n
FastAPI owns rules, data and alerts; n8n owns messaging and schedules.
- Backend → n8n: HMAC-signed webhooks on events (`mobilization.approved`, `approval.requested`,
  `assignment.started`, `alert.created`, `alert.escalated`).
- n8n → backend: a user with role `service_n8n` (`notification:write`, `report:read`,
  `alert:detect`).
- Workflows exported as JSON into `infra/n8n/`.
- **Schedules don't live in n8n on the free tier.** A GitHub Actions workflow
  (`.github/workflows/schedules.yml`) calls each scheduled workflow's webhook, HMAC-signed, which
  also wakes n8n; it retries with backoff for up to 3 minutes. UTC times: 07:00 Gulf time =
  `0 3 * * *`, 18:00 = `0 14 * * *`. GitHub can start scheduled runs late and disables them after
  60 days without repo activity, so "Run now" is the fallback and a missed run shows on the
  Automations screen.
- **Run now:** the admin Automations screen lists each scheduled workflow with its schedule and
  last run (from `audit_log`), plus "Run now" (`automation:run`, Super Admin).
- In production n8n is always on and its own cron takes over (`SCHEDULER=n8n`), with detectors
  hourly.

| Workflow | Trigger | Action |
|---|---|---|
| Detector run | 07:00 and 18:00 (scheduler) or Run now | Calls the API's detector run; then sends employee WhatsApp nudges for new 30-day and 7-day document alerts |
| Alert notify | `alert.created` (critical / action needed) | Notify the level-0 recipients (HR team) in-app + WhatsApp / email |
| Alert escalation | `alert.escalated` | Notify the next level: HR Manager, then Operations Manager |
| Approval request | `approval.requested` | Notify the approver. Reminders and escalation come from the `approval_waiting` alert |
| Mobilization tasks | `mobilization.approved` | Task per department with worker list |
| Arrival confirmation | `assignment.started` | Ask Site Supervisor to confirm arrival |
| Daily summary | 18:00 (scheduler) or Run now | Email CEO, Operations Manager, HR Manager, with alert counts by severity |

**Free-tier limit:** the API decides escalations when detectors run (events, 07:00, 18:00). A 4 h
critical step can therefore fire up to ~13 h late in the demo. Acceptable for the demo; production
runs detectors hourly.

## Summer midday work ban (UAE, MoHRE)
Outdoor work under direct sun banned 12:30–15:00 daily; 2026 window 15 Jun–15 Sep; max 8 working
hours/day during the ban. Stored in `work_rules` (one row per year, HR edits it; a provisional row
can be entered for planning and is labelled as such). Applies to sites with
`project_sites.outdoor = true`. Python validation rejects outdoor shifts overlapping the window or
exceeding 8 h (warns only against a provisional rule). The shift is
`employee_assignments.shift_start/shift_end` when set, otherwise the site's defaults. Exemptions
need a reason + Operations Manager approval. Demo has a "simulate date" control.

## AI agents
- Plain tool-calling loop (no agent framework) behind one `LLMClient` interface.
- Adapters: OpenAI-compatible (Groq, OpenRouter, Ollama) for the demo, which runs on **free
  models**; an Anthropic adapter for later. Swapping provider is a config change only.
- Model routing via config: provider + model per tier. `light` = routing, alert wording, column
  mapping; `mid` = staffing and forecast reasoning. Agents declare a tier, never a model name.
- Restricted fields (`doc_number`, contract value, phone numbers) are redacted before any model
  call; the model works with employee codes and the service layer resolves them.
- Free models are weaker at tool calling, so: strict JSON schemas, one retry on invalid output,
  then fail cleanly (`status = failed`). The Alert Narrator falls back to template text.
- Numbers are never computed by a model: forecasts, gaps, counts and dates come from Python.
- Token budget and max ~6 tool rounds per run. Log every run to `agent_runs`.
- Evals: 35 golden prompts in `apps/api/agents/evals/golden.yaml` (30 + 5 forecasting; 1 marked
  post-MVP). `make eval` reports pass rate, avg tokens, latency and cost. The narrator is covered
  by unit tests of the faithfulness check, not by evals.

## Build plan (phases; replaces the earlier 12-day and 22-day plans)

**Audience for the MVP: HR (Manager and Executive) and MD/CEO only.** Other teams come after the MVP.
Research behind the choices: `docs/hrms-research.md`.

**How to read it.** There are no day counts. Each phase has a goal, what ships, a gate that must pass
before the next phase starts, and what is cut if time is short. The quality bar from `CLAUDE.md` →
"Done means" is part of every phase, not a final pass: loading, empty, error and success states, motion
that follows `docs/design-direction.md`, keyboard support, light and dark, a 390px layout, seeded data,
no dead buttons, and screenshots at 1440px and 390px. A phase is not finished if any screen in it misses
the bar; that screen is cut instead.

### Phase 0: Foundation (done)
- **Goal:** a safe base to build on.
- **Ships:** repo, CI, production guard, tokens (red, white, black), design system with motion, API
  skeleton, clickable HR and CEO prototype (Home, To-do, Workers, Documents, Requests, Forecast,
  Projects, confirm dialogs), HR test kit.
- **Gate (passed):** CI green; contrast script passes; prototype tests green.
- **Cut:** not applicable.

### Phase 1: Validate with HR
- **Goal:** learn what confuses HR before building more.
- **Ships:** 3 to 5 HR sessions on the prototype using `docs/hr-test-kit.md`; fixes; the word HR uses
  for "mobilization" settled; copy, severity labels and the confirm dialog ported to real components
  with tests and gallery entries.
- **Gate:** at least 4 of 5 testers finish tasks 1, 2 and 4 without help; any word two testers
  misunderstand is changed.
- **Cut if short:** the second round of sessions. Never cut the first three sessions.

### Phase 2: Data spine
- **Goal:** real data and real rules behind the screens.
- **Ships:** Alembic baseline from `db/schema.sql`; seed that matches the prototype (150 workers, 112
  deployed, the document counts); authentication; RBAC with project scope; field masking; app shell with
  sidebar from permissions; hosted deploy.
- **Gate:** `make reset-demo` produces the seeded demo; HR Manager, HR Executive and MD/CEO sign in on the
  hosted site; masking and scope are proven by tests.
- **Cut if short:** the hosted deploy (stay local and demo from a laptop).

### Phase 3: People and allocation core (the demo-ready checkpoint)
- **Goal:** HR does the daily job on real data.
- **Ships:** Workers directory and profile; Documents by expiry window; staffing requests; Allocation
  board with hard-rule validators; approval with consequence preview; mobilization status; audit log.
- **Gate (demo-ready checkpoint):** on the hosted site, HR finds a worker, sees whose documents are
  running out, fills a request and approves it; the audit log shows who did what; results appear without
  a reload; the whole flow passes an end-to-end test.
- **Cut if short:** mobilization checklist detail, CSV export, Workers column options. The checkpoint
  itself is not cut.

### Phase 4: Foresight
- **Goal:** tell HR and the CEO what is coming.
- **Ships:** the seven detectors with auto-resolve; To-do with escalation and the bell; forecast service
  and screen (table first, then heatmap and Sankey); CEO overview showing exposure, gap and waiting
  approvals.
- **Gate:** each detector raises, updates and resolves under a fake clock; moving a project end date
  raises the transfer-dependency alert; CEO numbers match HR numbers.
- **Cut if short:** Sankey and heatmap polish.
- **Status:** built: the seven detectors (midday ban on a provisional 2027 rule), To-do with escalation,
  the bell, the Action Center on HR's Home, the MD/CEO alert tile, the forecast table, and "Change dates"
  on projects (which is how the transfer alert is shown live). Each gate item has a test: the detector
  tests under a fixed clock, `tests/test_alerts_api.py` and the live spec. Cut: heatmap and Sankey.
  Left for Phase 5 (n8n): sending messages, the 07:00 / 18:00 schedule (`POST /alerts/run` is ready),
  and the AI-written explanations (every alert is worded in Python for now).

### Phase 5: Automation and AI
- **Goal:** remove manual chasing.
- **Ships:** n8n flows: visa-expiry notices by email and WhatsApp, with HR's one-click "tell the worker"
  (design agreed with the user first) and department tasks after approval; Workforce agent suggestions
  with visible steps; Alert Narrator with the faithfulness check; Excel import with the Import Agent
  pre-filling the column mapping.
- **Gate:** a test approval sends a real WhatsApp or email to the test number; narrator numbers match the
  facts in every eval; the import sample with deliberate errors reports each bad row.
- **Cut if short:** the Import Agent (keep manual mapping); WhatsApp (keep email).
- **Order and status:** 5.1 AI runtime + Alert Narrator (**built**: model adapters, redaction, facts check,
  run log, narrator; runs only when a model is configured, `make ai-smoke` tries one), 5.2 Workforce agent with
  visible steps (**built**: "Ask Workforce agent" on a request line, "Explain" on a forecast, `make eval` with
  8 cases; the 35-case full-product suite is kept as `post-mvp.yaml`), 5.3 n8n messages and schedules, 5.4 Excel import. Each is verified against a scripted model
  and a local mail catcher until real keys and accounts exist.

### Phase 6: Hardening and handover
- **Goal:** safe to put in front of the CEO.
- **Ships:** full tests and evals; accessibility pass; security review; screenshots of every screen
  (1440 and 390, light and dark); two rehearsals; an honest "what is next" page (payroll, leave,
  attendance, other teams).
- **Gate:** the whole demo runs in about five minutes on the hosted site with no errors.
- **Cut:** nothing in this phase is cut; earlier phases give way first.

### Still out of scope
Dashboards for roles other than HR and MD/CEO, the Cmd+K command centre, demobilization, the Document
Intelligence agent, payroll, leave, attendance and camp management. Payroll and attendance are covered
by products such as Bayzat and TrueBays; the plan is to export to or integrate with them later.

**Cut order across phases if time runs short:** Phase 5 Import Agent, then Phase 4 chart polish, then
Phase 3 extras, then Phase 2 hosting. Phases 1, 3 and 6 are never cut.

## Path to production
Production needs configuration and infrastructure changes, not code rewrites. The production
guard (see `CLAUDE.md` → Environments) stops the API from starting with any demo-only setting.

**Production-ready as built (identical in demo and production):** RBAC and scope, field masking
and the Logistics projection, the hard-rule validators, approvals, the forecast service, alert
detectors and their lifecycle, the append-only audit, migrations (baseline `db/schema.sql`,
drift-checked in CI), the agent tool permission layer and redaction, HMAC-signed n8n webhooks,
signed file URLs, tests and evals.

| Document Intelligence | Not in the MVP: manual upload and HR verification | OCR + MRZ extraction with the Document Intelligence Agent (design in `docs/agents.md`), still HR-verified | Build the agent and enable it in the seed; the post-MVP eval case switches on. |
